About

I enjoy the part of engineering where things have to work physically: tuning a filter until a noisy EMG signal becomes usable, re-designing a bracket until it stops snapping, or tracing a fault with an oscilloscope until the waveform makes sense.

I also teach robotics to around 45 students a week, which has made me good at explaining technical ideas clearly and debugging other people's hardware quickly. Before university I worked as a biomedical engineering technician at King's College Hospital, carrying out preventive maintenance and electrical safety testing on clinical equipment.
